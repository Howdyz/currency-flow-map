Reading the map
What is being measured?

Every year the IRS matches tax returns to see who changed states. Each arrow is the adjusted gross income of the households that moved along that route between the 2022 and 2023 filing years. It's money that follows people: paychecks, business income and retirement income that now gets earned and spent somewhere else.

How to use it

Click any state to see where its money went and where its newcomers came from. Orange arrows leave the state, green arrows arrive. Click a state in the list to jump to it. Drag to pan, scroll or pinch to zoom, and press space to blink the heat layer off and on. Table ranks every state and route, and Share copies a link that reopens exactly what you're looking at.

What it leaves out

Only households that filed taxes both years are counted, so it misses people who don't file and money that moves without anyone moving (online sales, investments, federal spending). For each state the map keeps its six biggest routes in and out, which covers most of the dollars.

Data: IRS SOI migration data, 2022–2023
